A cozy spot-the-difference game set on an Indonesian morning: a warung kopi stall, a bustling pasar pagi, and breakfast on the terrace — three hand-drawn scenes (all art drawn in code, no photos), 7 + 10 + 12 differences to hunt. Two near-identical pictures side by side; click a difference on either panel to circle it in red, watch the counter tick down, spend one of three hints per level to pulse a hidden difference, and beat the clock for a time bonus. Wrong clicks cost points. Keyboard: H hint, R restart, 1/2/3 pick a level, Space to play.

## Run it

node serve.ts
